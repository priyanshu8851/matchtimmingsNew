IPL

The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a professional men's Twenty20 cricket league, contested by ten teams based out of ten Indian cities. Founded in 2007 by the BCCI, it is held annually between March and May.

The IPL is one of the most popular cricket leagues globally and has revolutionized the sport with its franchise model, entertainment value, and global fan base.
